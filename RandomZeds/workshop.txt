description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Set probabilities for Crawlers, Shamblers, Fast Shamblers, and Sprinters.
description=[*]Use separate Day, Night, and Weather profiles.
description=[*]Set seasonal Day and Night schedules.
description=[*]Configure toughness, sight, and hearing for each speed type.
description=[*]Set a Sprinter base speed with separate decrease and increase limits.
description=[*]Reroll loaded zombies when settings change.
description=[*]Protect nearby Crawlers from speed-type changes.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Setup[/h2]
description=Open the Sandbox Options and configure the Random Zeds Main, Day, Night, and Weather pages.
description=
description=[h2]For mod developers[/h2]
description=Set [b]zombie:getModData().RandomZedsExcluded = true[/b] to exclude a zombie from all Random Zeds changes and synchronization.
