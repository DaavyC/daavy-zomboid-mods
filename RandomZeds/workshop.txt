description=[h1]Random Zeds[/h1]
[b]Random Zeds[/b] lets you configure different zombie types and toughness levels using custom probabilities.
[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Configure the chance of Crawlers, Shamblers, Fast Shamblers, and Sprinters.
[*]Separate profiles for Day and Night.
[*]Special profile for Rain, Fog, and Snow.
[*]Set the toughness of each zombie type to Fragile, Normal, or Tough.
[*]Adjust Sprinter speed from 0.5x to 1.5x.
[*]Loaded zombies can be rerolled when the settings change.
[*]Crawlers near players will not change into other speed types.
[/list]
[h2]How to Use[/h2]
Enable the mod and open the Sandbox Options:
[list]
[*]Random Zeds - Day
[*]Random Zeds - Night
[*]Random Zeds - Weather
[/list]
Configure the probabilities based on your preferred difficulty level.
[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[b]Project Zomboid Build 42.20.X or later.[/b]
