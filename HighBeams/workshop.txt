description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Only the driver can switch modes. The option sits next to the vanilla headlight control.
description=[*]By default, high beams reach twice as far and are 1.5 times as bright as normal headlights.
description=[*]Adjust distance and brightness in Sandbox Options.
description=[*]Each vehicle keeps its selected mode after a server restart.
description=[*]Players see the same headlight mode in multiplayer.
description=[*]The mod is compatible with vanilla and modded vehicles.
description=[/list]
