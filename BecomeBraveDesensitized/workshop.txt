description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Kill zombies to earn Brave and Desensitized.
description=[*]Fear traits raise the kill requirement. Choose whether they block Brave. You can configure Reluctant Fighter to raise the requirement, but it never blocks Brave.
description=[*]Earning either trait removes Cowardly, Agoraphobic, Claustrophobic, and Fear of Blood. Earning Desensitized also removes Adrenaline Junkie.
description=[*]Hunter and Target Shooter reduce the kill requirements for both traits. Brave reduces only the requirement for Desensitized.
description=[*]Doctor, Lumberjack, Nurse, Park Ranger, Police Officer, and Security Guard each reduce the kill requirements for both traits.
description=[*]Enable Early Rewards for a chance to earn either trait before you reach the required kill count.
description=[/list]
