title=Random Zeds
description=[h1]Random Zeds[/h1]
description=[b]Random Zeds[/b] lets you configure different zombie types and toughness levels using custom probabilities.
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Configure the chance of Crawlers, Shamblers, Fast Shamblers, and Sprinters.
description=[*]Separate profiles for Day and Night.
description=[*]Special profile for Rain, Fog, and Snow.
description=[*]Set the toughness of each zombie type to Fragile, Normal, or Tough.
description=[*]Set Sprinter base speed from 0.5x to 1.5x and add individual speed variation.
description=[*]Loaded zombies can be rerolled when the settings change.
description=[*]Crawlers near players will not change into other speed types.
description=[/list]
description=[h2]How to Use[/h2]
description=Enable the mod and open the Sandbox Options:
description=[list]
description=[*]Random Zeds - Main
description=[*]Random Zeds - Day
description=[*]Random Zeds - Night
description=[*]Random Zeds - Weather
description=[/list]
description=Configure the probabilities based on your preferred difficulty level.
